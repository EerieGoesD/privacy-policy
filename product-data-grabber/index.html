Product Data Grabber Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Product Data Grabber ("the app") is a desktop app from EERIE that reads a product page from a supported online store and gives you its text and its picture addresses in a form you can select, copy and save, and that compares the prices of a product across several online stores. The stores it reads product pages from are Amazon, Aosom, Auchan, Continente, Decathlon, HSN, Naturitas, Nutribio, PcComponentes, Pingo Doce, Primor, Thomann, Worten and Zumub. The stores it compares prices across are Auchan, Bulk, Celeiro, Continente, Decathlon, HSN, MyProtein, Naturitas, Nutribio, Pingo Doce, Primor, Prozis, Vitalabo and Zumub. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours. It is not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated with any of these stores.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising. EERIE never receives the addresses you paste, the products you read or search for, the files you save, or anything else about your computer.

What the app reads

When you press Get Product, the app opens the address you pasted in its own browser window, off screen, and reads the text already published on that page. On Decathlon that is the product title, description, product number, colour, features, design details, specifications, and the addresses of the product photographs. On Amazon it is the product title, the short overview table, the "About this product" list, the product information tables, and the addresses of the product photographs. On Aosom it is the product title, the highlights with the brand, SKU and pack quantity, the weights and dimensions, the characteristics, the returns and business panels, the details text, and the addresses of the product photographs. On PcComponentes it is the product title, the summary of highlights, the product description, and the addresses of the product photographs. On Worten it is the product title, the technical characteristics, the seller's description and the manufacturer where the page has them, and the addresses of the product photographs. On Zumub it is the product title, the description, the technical sheet, the nutrition and directions, and the addresses of the product photographs. On Naturitas it is the product title, a summary with the pack size, the short description, the prices, the stock and the delivery dates, the full description, the nutrition table, the technical sheet, and the addresses of the product photographs. On Primor it is the product title with the line under it, the details, the characteristics, the directions, the ingredients, and the addresses of the product photographs. On Nutribio it is the product title, a summary with the price, the short description, the reference, the categories and the brand, the description, and the addresses of the product photographs. On HSN it is the product title, the short description, the product information list, and for the flavour or size in the address its recommended use, nutrition table, ingredients, warnings and the addresses of its photographs. On Pingo Doce it is the product title, a summary with the brand, the size and the price, the description, nutrition and legal data tabs, and the addresses of the product photographs. On Continente it is the product title, a summary with the brand, the size and the prices, the additional, nutrition and legal information boxes, which the page asks the store's website for when they are opened and the app asks for the same way, and the addresses of the product photographs. On Auchan it is the product title, a summary with the reference, the size and the prices, the details, the characteristics, the description, and the addresses of the product photographs. On Thomann it is the product title, the description, the list of features with their values, and the addresses of the product photographs. That text stays on your computer, in the app window, until you close the app or read another product. The app does not save it by itself.

Price comparison

When you type what to look for and press Compare Prices, the app opens the search page of each of the comparison stores ticked under Shops (from Auchan, Bulk, Celeiro, Continente, Decathlon, HSN, MyProtein, Naturitas, Nutribio, Pingo Doce, Primor, Prozis, Vitalabo and Zumub) for those words, each in its own browser window, off screen, and reads the products, sizes and prices the search shows. On Auchan, Continente and Pingo Doce it also reads the further pages of results, asking the store's website for them the same way its "Ver mais" button does. Where the search does not give enough to compare by, the app also reads the pages of the products found, and on Naturitas and Primor their multi-buy pack prices and discount codes, from the store's own website the same way its pages show them to you. For a protein search it reads the page of every product found, for the protein in each scoop. When a product's weight is still not known after that, the app downloads the product's photos, at the addresses the store's own page gives for them, and reads the weight or the dose printed on the pack with a text reader built into the app, on your computer. Nothing is sent to any text-reading service. The photos themselves are not kept. Each store therefore receives the words you typed, the same as if you searched its website in your browser. The results stay on your computer, in the app window, and are not saved by the app unless you press Save table for later. That keeps one table, with the words you searched for, in the app's own storage on your computer, so Load saved table can show it again without searching the stores. Saving again replaces it, and removing the app removes it. It is never sent anywhere.

So that the same pages and photos are not downloaded on every comparison, the app keeps what it has read on your computer: the passages about amounts and doses from each product page, in the app's own browser storage for that store's website, and the words read from each product photo, in the app's own storage. A product page is read again when the store's search shows a new price for it, or after 30 days. Nothing else about the pages is kept, removing the app removes all of it, and none of it is ever sent anywhere.

Network use

The app is not offline software. Opening a product page is an ordinary visit to that store's website, made from your computer, so the store's servers see it the same way they would see a visit from your web browser, including your IP address. Before opening an Amazon address, the app removes the advertising and tracking parts that addresses copied from adverts and search results carry, keeping only the product code, the page language and the choice of product variation.

A price comparison is likewise an ordinary search on each comparison store's website, made from your computer.

Pressing Save pictures downloads the photographs from the store's own picture server (media-amazon.com or ssl-images-amazon.com for Amazon, aosomcdn.com for Aosom, mediadecathlon.com for Decathlon, img.pccomponentes.com for PcComponentes, Worten's own website for Worten, zumub.com for Zumub, Naturitas's own picture servers on cloudfront.net and naturitas.es for Naturitas, primor.eu for Primor, nutribio.pt for Nutribio, HSN's own website for HSN, pingodoce.pt for Pingo Doce, continente.pt for Continente, auchan.pt for Auchan, and thomann.de and static-thomann.de for Thomann) to the folder you choose. The app only opens addresses on the supported stores, only downloads pictures from those picture servers and, during a price comparison, the product photos the comparison stores' own pages point to, and contacts nothing else. It makes no connection at all when you simply open it.

Cookies from the stores' websites

Because each product page is loaded in a real browser window inside the app, the store's website can set cookies exactly as it would in your normal browser. Those cookies are stored by Windows in the app's own browser folder on your computer, are used only for loading these pages, and are never read or transmitted by the app. Removing the app removes them.

Where your settings are stored

Your settings are saved on your own computer: the light or dark theme, whether the app usage readout under the title is shown, which of the two screens you used last, which comparison stores are searched, the delivery fees you enter for the comparison stores and the price from which each delivers for free, which column the comparison is ordered by, whether out of stock products are filtered out of the comparison, the products you block there, the words you type to hide results from it, and the size and position of the app's window so it reopens the way you left it. The addresses you paste are not saved anywhere and are gone when you close the app.

Files you save

Save as spreadsheet writes a CSV file, and Save pictures writes image files, to a location you pick each time. Those files stay on your device and under your control. The app never uploads them.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party trackers, advertising SDKs, or analytics libraries. The only third parties it ever contacts are the supported stores' own websites and picture servers, and only for the pages and searches you asked for. What a store does with that visit is covered by that store's own privacy policy, not this one. Links inside the app, such as "Support This Project", "Report Issue", "Discussions", "Feature Request" and the link to suggest another store, open in your web browser only when you click them, taking you to third-party websites that operate under their own privacy policies.

Third-party distribution

If the app is obtained through the Microsoft Store or another platform, that platform may process data related to download, purchase, and licensing under its own policy. This policy describes only what the Product Data Grabber app itself does.

Children's privacy

Product Data Grabber does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
