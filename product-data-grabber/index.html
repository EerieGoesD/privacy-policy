Product Data Grabber Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Product Data Grabber ("the app") is a desktop app from EERIE that reads a product page from a supported online store and gives you its text and its picture addresses in a form you can select, copy and save, and that compares the prices of a product across several online stores. The stores it reads product pages from are Amazon, Aosom, Decathlon, Naturitas, PcComponentes, Primor, Worten and Zumub. The stores it compares prices across are Bulk, Celeiro, Decathlon, HSN, MyProtein, Naturitas, Nutribio, Primor, Prozis, Vitalabo and Zumub. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours. It is not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated with any of these stores.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising. EERIE never receives the addresses you paste, the products you read or search for, the files you save, or anything else about your computer.

What the app reads

When you press Get Product, the app opens the address you pasted in its own browser window, off screen, and reads the text already published on that page. On Decathlon that is the product title, description, product number, colour, features, design details, specifications, and the addresses of the product photographs. On Amazon it is the product title, the short overview table, the "About this product" list, the product information tables, and the addresses of the product photographs. On Aosom it is the product title, the highlights with the brand, SKU and pack quantity, the weights and dimensions, the characteristics, the returns and business panels, the details text, and the addresses of the product photographs. On PcComponentes it is the product title, the summary of highlights, the product description, and the addresses of the product photographs. On Worten it is the product title, the technical characteristics, the seller's description and the manufacturer where the page has them, and the addresses of the product photographs. On Zumub it is the product title, the description, the technical sheet, the nutrition and directions, and the addresses of the product photographs. On Naturitas it is the product title, a summary with the pack size, the short description, the prices, the stock and the delivery dates, the full description, the nutrition table, the technical sheet, and the addresses of the product photographs. On Primor it is the product title with the line under it, the details, the characteristics, the directions, the ingredients, and the addresses of the product photographs. That text stays on your computer, in the app window, until you close the app or read another product. The app does not save it by itself.

Price comparison

When you type what to look for and press Compare Prices, the app opens the search page of each of the comparison stores (Bulk, Celeiro, Decathlon, HSN, MyProtein, Naturitas, Nutribio, Primor, Prozis, Vitalabo and Zumub) for those words, each in its own browser window, off screen, and reads the products, sizes and prices the search shows. Where the search does not give enough to compare by, the app also reads the pages of the products found, and on Naturitas and Primor their multi-buy pack prices and discount codes, from the store's own website the same way its pages show them to you. Each store therefore receives the words you typed, the same as if you searched its website in your browser. The results stay on your computer, in the app window, and are not saved by the app.

Network use

The app is not offline software. Opening a product page is an ordinary visit to that store's website, made from your computer, so the store's servers see it the same way they would see a visit from your web browser, including your IP address. Before opening an Amazon address, the app removes the advertising and tracking parts that addresses copied from adverts and search results carry, keeping only the product code, the page language and the choice of product variation.

A price comparison is likewise an ordinary search on each comparison store's website, made from your computer.

Pressing Save pictures downloads the photographs from the store's own picture server (media-amazon.com or ssl-images-amazon.com for Amazon, aosomcdn.com for Aosom, mediadecathlon.com for Decathlon, img.pccomponentes.com for PcComponentes, Worten's own website for Worten, zumub.com for Zumub, Naturitas's own picture server on cloudfront.net for Naturitas, and primor.eu for Primor) to the folder you choose. The app only opens addresses on the supported stores, only downloads pictures from those picture servers, and contacts nothing else. It makes no connection at all when you simply open it.

Cookies from the stores' websites

Because each product page is loaded in a real browser window inside the app, the store's website can set cookies exactly as it would in your normal browser. Those cookies are stored by Windows in the app's own browser folder on your computer, are used only for loading these pages, and are never read or transmitted by the app. Removing the app removes them.

Where your settings are stored

Your settings are saved on your own computer: the light or dark theme, whether the app usage readout under the title is shown, which of the two screens you used last, the delivery fees you enter for the comparison stores, and the size and position of the app's window so it reopens the way you left it. The addresses you paste are not saved anywhere and are gone when you close the app.

Files you save

Save as spreadsheet writes a CSV file, and Save pictures writes image files, to a location you pick each time. Those files stay on your device and under your control. The app never uploads them.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party trackers, advertising SDKs, or analytics libraries. The only third parties it ever contacts are the supported stores' own websites and picture servers, and only for the pages and searches you asked for. What a store does with that visit is covered by that store's own privacy policy, not this one. Links inside the app, such as "Support This Project", "Report Issue", "Discussions", "Feature Request" and the link to suggest another store, open in your web browser only when you click them, taking you to third-party websites that operate under their own privacy policies.

Third-party distribution

If the app is obtained through the Microsoft Store or another platform, that platform may process data related to download, purchase, and licensing under its own policy. This policy describes only what the Product Data Grabber app itself does.

Children's privacy

Product Data Grabber does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
