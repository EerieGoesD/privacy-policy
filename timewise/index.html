Timewise Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Timewise ("the app") is an app from EERIE for running countdowns, stopwatches, Pomodoro and interval timers, counters and clocks side by side. Everything you create is kept on your own device. If you choose to switch on sync, a copy of your timers is also kept on a server we run, so your other devices show the same timers.

What we collect

Nothing unless you switch on sync. With sync off the app never contacts us and we hold no data about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no telemetry and no tracking.

Where your data lives

Your boards, timers, their settings, notes and history, and the app's own settings are stored on your device, in the app's own storage folder.

Sync

Sync is off unless you turn it on in Settings. Turning it on gives you a random 16 character sync code. Typing that code into Timewise on another device shows the same timers there.

While sync is on, the app keeps a copy of your boards and timers on a server we run on Cloudflare, together with the time it was last saved. That is all that is stored. We do not store your name, your email address, your device details or your location. There is no account and no password: the sync code is the only thing that ties the copy to you, so keep it to yourself as you would a password.

Turning sync off deletes the copy from the server. Your device keeps everything it has. You can also email us at the address below with your sync code and we will delete the copy.

Notifications

The first time a running timer has an alert coming up, the app asks your device for permission to show notifications. Alerts for timers that finish while the app is closed are scheduled with your own device, and on an iPhone they can also appear on a paired Apple Watch. Nothing about your timers is sent to us to deliver them.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether the trial or the purchase is active. We do not receive your payment details or your Apple ID.

Clipboard

When you press the copy button next to your sync code, the app writes that code to your clipboard. It never reads your clipboard.

Network use

With sync switched off the app works fully offline and does not connect to any server. Connections are opened only while sync is on and your timers are being kept in step, when the App Store checks or completes a purchase, or when you choose to click a link, such as the footer links, which opens that page in your default browser under that site's own privacy policy.

What we don't collect

The app does not collect analytics, telemetry, usage data, or device information. The memory and processor figures it can show at the bottom of the window are read from your own device and never leave it. It does not use advertising SDKs. We do not sell or share your data with anyone, and we do not use it to build a profile of you.

Children's privacy

Timewise is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Third-party distribution

If the app is obtained through the Apple App Store, Microsoft Store, Google Play or another platform, the respective platform may process data related to download, purchase, and licensing under their own policies. This policy describes only processing performed by the Timewise app itself.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
