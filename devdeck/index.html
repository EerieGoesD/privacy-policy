DevDeck Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

DevDeck ("the app") is a desktop app from EERIE for tracking the outstanding work across the projects you maintain. Everything you create stays on your own device, or in a folder you choose. The app has no accounts, no servers, and does not collect any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. DevDeck does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no sign-up, no login, and no user profile. The one thing that ever leaves your device is your own data file, and only if you switch sync on yourself (see Sync below).

Where your data lives

Your projects, tasks, sub-tasks, notes, dates, templates and app settings are stored locally on your device, in the app's own storage folder, together with two rolling backup copies the app keeps for you. This data is never sent to us. Settings shows you the exact folder and can open it, and lets you move the data file to a folder of your choosing (see below).

Sync

Sync is off unless you turn it on. When you turn it on, the app gives you a sync code and keeps a copy of your data file on a server we run on Cloudflare, so your other devices can show the same list. There is no account, no email address and no password: the code is the only thing that identifies your data, which is why the app tells you to treat it like a password. We store only the data file itself and the time it was last saved. We do not store your name, your email address, your device details or your location. Turning sync off deletes the copy from the server. You can also email us and we will delete it.

Using DevDeck on more than one computer

Settings lets you choose a different folder to keep your data file in, so another computer running DevDeck can open the same list. If you pick a folder that a cloud drive such as iCloud Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox syncs for you, that provider will store and transfer the file under its own privacy policy and terms. This is entirely your choice, it is off unless you turn it on, and you can switch back to this computer only at any time. DevDeck itself has no server and never uploads anything.

Backups

Export writes a backup file to a location you choose, and Import reads a backup file you select. The app can also export a project's task list as a markdown file to a location you choose. These files stay on your device and under your control. The app never uploads them.

Reminders

When you give a task a reminder, the app asks the operating system for permission to show notifications and then shows the reminder on your own device. Nothing about the task is sent anywhere.

Clipboard

When you press the copy button next to a note, the app writes that text to your clipboard. It never reads your clipboard, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Network use

With sync switched off the app works fully offline and does not connect to any server. Its fonts are bundled inside the app, so no web fonts are fetched. Connections are opened only when you switch sync on, or when you choose to click a link, such as the footer links or a link you wrote in a task's notes, which opens that page in your default browser under that site's own privacy policy.

What we don't collect

The app does not collect analytics, telemetry, usage data, or device information. It does not use advertising SDKs.

Third-party distribution

If the app is obtained through the Microsoft Store, Apple App Store, or another platform, the respective platform may process data related to download, purchase, and licensing under their own policies. This policy describes only processing performed by the DevDeck app itself.
