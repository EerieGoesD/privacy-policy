Bear in Mind Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Bear in Mind ("the app") is a desktop app from EERIE for keeping a list of the things you are afraid of forgetting. Everything you write is kept on your own computer.

What we collect

Nothing. Bear in Mind has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no telemetry and no tracking. We hold no data about you at all.

Where your data lives

The things you add, their descriptions, whether each one is ticked off, and your settings are saved in two plain files in the app's own storage folder on your computer. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to reach it.

Import and export

Export writes a copy of your list to a file wherever you choose to put it. Import reads a file you pick and adds what is in it to your list. Both files stay on your computer and under your control. The app never uploads either one.

The usage readout

Under the app's name Bear in Mind shows how much processor time and memory the app itself is using, and how much space its own files take up. These figures are measured on your computer, shown on screen, and never sent anywhere. They can be switched off in the settings.

Network use

The app works fully offline and never contacts anything on its own. The only time a connection is opened is when you click one of the links at the bottom of the window, which opens that page in your default browser under that site's own privacy policy.

What we don't collect

The app collects no analytics, no telemetry, no usage data and no device information. It contains no third-party trackers, advertising components or analytics libraries. We do not sell or share your data with anyone, and we do not use it to build a profile of you.

Third parties

Bear in Mind shares no data with third parties, because it collects none.

Children's privacy

Bear in Mind does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Third-party distribution

If the app is obtained through the Microsoft Store or another platform, that platform may process data related to download, purchase and licensing under its own policies. This policy describes only what the Bear in Mind app itself does.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
