Bear in Mind Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

Bear in Mind ("the app") is an app from EERIE for keeping a list of the things you are afraid of forgetting. It comes as a desktop app, an Android app, a web version at eeriegoesd.com/bear-in-mind and a browser extension. This policy covers all four. Everything you write is kept on your own computer or phone, in whichever of them you are using. If you choose to create an account in the desktop app, the Android app or the web version, a copy of your list is also kept on a server we run, so your other devices show the same list.

What we collect

Without an account: from the desktop app, the web version and the browser extension, nothing. From the Android app, one small record used only for the free trial: a scrambled identifier for your phone and the date and time the trial was started on it (see "Free trial and unlocking the app" below). Without an account we never receive anything you write in the app.

If you create an account, we hold your email address, a scrambled form of your password, and a copy of your list. The account is optional and the app works fully without one.

Bear in Mind itself has no analytics, no telemetry and no tracking. The web version is a page of eeriegoesd.com, so the website's own privacy page covers the site's analytics, which run only if you accept its cookie banner and never include anything you write.

Where your data lives

The things you add, their descriptions, whether each one is ticked off, and your settings are saved on your own device. In the desktop app and the Android app they are two plain files in the app's own storage. In the web version they are kept in your browser's own storage for eeriegoesd.com, on your machine. In the browser extension they are kept in the browser's own storage for that extension, which stays on your machine and is not shared with any web page you visit. Unless you sign in to an account (below), nothing you write is uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to reach it.

On Android, if your phone's own backup is turned on, Android may include the app's files in your Google backup so they can come back on a new phone. That backup is made by Android and kept by Google under Google's privacy policy; the app does not send it anywhere itself.

Without an account, the desktop app, the Android app, the web version and the extension each keep a separate list. With an account, the desktop app, the Android app and the web version show the same list wherever you are signed in. The browser extension has no account and always keeps its own list. Moving a list without an account is done by exporting from one and importing into the other.

Accounts

An account is optional. It exists so that signing in on another device brings your list with you. To create one you give an email address and pick a password.

Your password never leaves your device. The app scrambles it on your own device first, and only the scrambled result is sent to us. What we keep is a further scrambled form of that, so the password itself cannot be worked out from anything we hold, by us or by anyone who obtained a copy of our records.

We keep your email address, that scrambled password, the date the account was made, whether the address has been confirmed, and an internal reference that ties the account to your saved list. We do not keep your name, your device details, your location, or your IP address.

Emails we send

We send email only to confirm your address when you sign up, and to send a code if you ask to reset or change your password. Both are six digit codes that stop working after fifteen minutes. We do not send newsletters, marketing or any other mail. These messages are sent through Resend, an email delivery service, which handles your address under its own privacy policy in order to deliver them.

Your list on the server

When you are signed in, the app keeps a copy of your list (each item, its description, whether it is ticked off, when it was added and finished, and any reminder time set on Android) on a server we run on Cloudflare, and stores the time it was last saved. Your settings are not sent. That copy is encrypted before it is written, with a key held only by our service and never stored alongside the data, so the stored records are unreadable on their own. Our service can decrypt it in order to hand it back to your signed-in devices. Your email address is not encrypted, because it has to be looked up when you sign in.

Signing out on a device stops that device keeping in step and leaves your other devices working. It does not delete the copy on the server. Delete Account in Settings, which asks for your password, deletes the account and everything stored with it. You can also email us at the address below to have it deleted.

Import and export

Export writes a copy of your list to a file: the desktop app and the Android app ask you where to put it, and the extension saves it to your downloads. Import reads a file you pick and adds what is in it to your list. Both files stay on your device and under your control. The app never uploads them.

Free trial and unlocking the app

The Android app offers a 15-day free trial and then a one-time purchase for permanent access. The purchase is handled by Google Play. The app asks Google Play whether you have bought the unlock. We do not receive your name, your payment details or your Google account details, and no purchase information is sent to any server of ours.

So that the trial cannot be restarted by reinstalling the app, the date the trial started is kept on a server we run on Cloudflare. It is stored next to a scrambled identifier for your phone: a one-way hash made on the phone from the identifier Android gives this app on that device (the Android ID). The Android ID itself never leaves your phone, and the hash cannot be turned back into it. The record holds no name, email address, Google account or anything you have written, and it is used for nothing except the trial. Cloudflare, which runs the server, handles the connection under its own privacy policy.

We keep this record so the trial is not given twice on the same phone. Because it holds nothing that identifies you, we have no way to tell from a request which record belongs to whom.

The desktop app is sold through the Microsoft Store and has no trial or in-app purchase. The browser extension has neither.

The usage readout

Under the app's name the desktop app and the Android app show how much processor time and memory the app is using, and how much space its own files take up. The browser extension shows only how much room its stored list takes up, because a browser gives an extension no way to measure processor or memory use. These figures are measured on your device, shown on screen, and never sent anywhere. They can be switched off in the settings.

Network use

With no account signed in, the desktop app, the web version and the extension work fully offline and never contact anything on their own. The extension reads no web page, asks for no access to any site, and runs nothing that is not inside its own package.

The Android app works offline too. It opens a connection to check the trial and the purchase: to Google Play, and to our server for the trial's start date. It does this when it opens, when you come back to it, and from time to time while you use it, and when you start the trial, buy, or restore a purchase.

When you are signed in, the desktop app, the Android app and the web version also connect to our server to keep your list in step: a few seconds after you change something, when you come back to the app, and when you sign up, sign in, confirm your address, reset or change your password, sign out or delete the account.

In all of them, a connection is also opened when you tap or click one of the links in the app, which opens that page in your browser under that site's own privacy policy.

What we don't collect

Apart from the trial record and, if you create one, the account described above, the app collects no analytics, no telemetry, no usage data and no device information. It contains no third-party trackers, advertising components or analytics libraries. We do not sell or share your data with anyone, and we do not use it to build a profile of you.

Third parties

In the Android app, Google Play handles the purchase. Cloudflare runs the server that keeps the trial record and, if you create an account, the account and the copy of your list. Resend delivers the account emails. Each works under its own privacy policy. Bear in Mind shares nothing else with anyone.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about your account, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing the address below. Deleting the account removes the email address, the scrambled password and the stored copy of your list. The copy on your own device is yours and is not affected.

Children's privacy

Bear in Mind is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Without an account it asks for no personal information at all, and the Android trial record holds no name, contact details or account.

Third-party distribution

If the app is obtained through the Microsoft Store, Google Play, the Chrome Web Store or another platform, that platform may process data related to download, install, purchase and licensing under its own policies. This policy describes only what Bear in Mind itself does.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
