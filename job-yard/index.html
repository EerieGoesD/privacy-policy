Job Yard Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Job Yard ("the app") is a desktop app from EERIE that searches job websites in Portugal, recruitment agencies, language job sites, and remote and freelance job boards for the words you type, and lists the jobs they find in one place. The sites it can search are Net-Empregos, SAPO Emprego, Expresso Emprego, Alerta Emprego, OLX Emprego, Indeed, LinkedIn, Jooble, Careerjet, Talent.com, Jobted, IEFP, EURES, ITJobs, Landing.jobs, Teamlyzer, Carga de Trabalhos, Turijobs, Hosco, Randstad, Adecco, ManpowerGroup, Hays, Michael Page, Europe Language Jobs, Teleperformance, Concentrix, Foundever, Eurojobs, Remote OK, Remotive, Working Nomads, Himalayas, We Work Remotely, Jobspresso, Remote Rocketship, NoDesk, Dynamite Jobs, Wellfound, JustRemote, Arc, Remote.co, Upwork and Freelancer. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours. It is not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated with any of these sites.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising. EERIE never receives the words you search for, the location you choose, the jobs you find or open, or anything else about your device.

What the app reads

When you press Search, the app searches each site switched on under Settings, Sites, for the words you typed and the location you picked. For most sites it opens the site's own search page in a browser window of its own, kept out of sight, and reads the jobs that page lists: the job title, the company, the location, the date, the salary where shown, a short summary, the job's tags, and the address of the job's own page. On IEFP it fills in the site's own search form with your words and sends it, the way you would; on NoDesk it types your words into the site's own search box; on Adecco and Europe Language Jobs it clicks each job in the list to read its address. Depending on the Pages per site setting, it also reads the further pages of results, pausing a few seconds between them. For ITJobs, Remote OK, Remotive, Working Nomads, Himalayas, We Work Remotely, Jobspresso and Freelancer, it reads the public job list each of those sites publishes for programs to read, instead of opening a page. Each site therefore receives the words you typed and, where the site searches by place, the location you picked, the same as if you searched its website in your browser.

Opening a job from the list opens that job's page in your default web browser.

Saved results

So that the same search within a day does not ask the sites again, the app keeps the jobs each site found for a search, together with the words and the location searched, in a file in the app's own folder on your device. They are shown again for the same search for 24 hours, after which the sites are searched again. Files older than two days are deleted the next time a search is saved, and removing the app removes them all. They are never sent anywhere.

Network use

The app is not offline software. Searching a site is an ordinary visit to that site's website, made from your device, so the site's servers see it the same way they would see a visit from your web browser, including your IP address. The app only opens addresses on the sites listed above and contacts nothing else. If a site asks for your location, to send notifications, or to use the camera or microphone, the app refuses at once without asking you, so none of these is ever shared. The app makes no connection at all when you simply open it.

Cookies from the sites

Because each search page is loaded in a real browser window inside the app, the site can set cookies exactly as it would in your normal browser. Those cookies are stored by the operating system in the app's own browser storage on your device, are used only for loading these pages, and are never read or transmitted by the app. Removing the app removes them.

Where your settings are stored

Your settings are saved on your own device: the light or dark theme, whether the app usage readout at the bottom of the window is shown, which sites are switched on, how many pages per site are read, the last words and location you searched for, the order the jobs are listed in, the work mode, salary and hidden-words filters, the time of the last search (so the app can wait half a minute before the next one), and the size and position of the app's window so it reopens the way you left it.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party trackers, advertising SDKs, or analytics libraries. The only third parties it ever contacts are the job sites listed above, and only for the searches you asked for. What a site does with that visit is covered by that site's own privacy policy, not this one. Links inside the app, such as "Support This Project", "Report Issue", "Discussions", "Feature Request", a site's name in the list of sites searched, and each job in the results, open in your web browser only when you click them, taking you to third-party websites that operate under their own privacy policies.

Third-party distribution

If the app is obtained through the Microsoft Store or another platform, that platform may process data related to download, purchase, and licensing under its own policy. This policy describes only what the Job Yard app itself does.

Children's privacy

Job Yard does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
