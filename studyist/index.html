Studyist Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Studyist ("the app") is a desktop app from EERIE for planning what you are studying: cards of topics and tickboxes arranged on a board, with screenshots pinned beside them. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no advertising.

What we collect

Nothing. Studyist has no user accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising. EERIE never sees your board, your notes, or anything else about your computer.

Where your data is stored

Your board, the pictures you paste or add, and your settings are saved in the app's own storage folder on your computer. Settings include the light or dark theme, the lettering and text size, the colours you have used, the board's zoom level and, if you use the optional AI feature, your chosen AI provider, model and API key. Nothing leaves your device except as described under "Optional sync" and "Optional AI help" below.

Optional sync

Studyist can keep the same board on more than one of your own computers. Sync is switched off until you turn it on yourself in Settings.

When it is on, the app creates a 16 character code. Your board and the pictures on it are copied to a small service run by EERIE at eerie-apps-sync.eeriegoesd.workers.dev, stored against that code, and copied back down to any device where you type the same code. What is stored is only the board file itself, the pictures on it, and the time each was last saved. No name, no email address, no device details, no location, and no account of any kind.

The code is the only thing protecting that copy, so anyone you give it to can read and change your board. Treat it like a password. Turning sync off in Settings deletes the copy from the service straight away, and everything stays on your own device.

Optional AI help

Studyist includes an optional "Ask AI" feature that is switched off until you set it up yourself. It is a "bring your own key" feature: you choose the AI provider and paste your own API key from that provider. EERIE does not operate an AI service, does not supply a key, and receives nothing from these requests.

When, and only when, you press Ask, the app sends the following directly from your computer to the provider you selected: the question you typed, and, if you leave that box ticked, the cards you chose, meaning their names, the lines on them, their notes and which lines are ticked. Any follow-up message you type in the AI panel is sent to the same provider along with the earlier messages of that conversation.

The supported providers are Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, and any other service you point the app at through the OpenAI-compatible option. Once your data reaches a provider it is handled under that provider's own privacy policy and terms, not this one. The app sends nothing to any AI provider until you have entered a key and pressed the button, and it never sends anything to EERIE.

Your API key is stored locally on your device so you do not have to paste it every time. It is sent only to the provider it belongs to, as part of the request. You can remove it at any time by clearing the field in Settings.

Network use

With sync switched off and no AI key entered, the app works fully offline and does not connect to any server. Links inside the app, such as "Support This Project", "Report Issue", "Discussions" and "Feature Request", open in your web browser only when you click them, taking you to third-party websites that operate under their own privacy policies.

Exports

Exporting writes a Markdown file of your board to a location you choose. That file stays on your device and under your control. The app never uploads it.

Third parties

Studyist contains no third-party trackers, advertising SDKs, or analytics libraries. The only third party that ever receives data is the AI provider you choose yourself, and only for the requests described above.

Third-party distribution

If the app is obtained through the Microsoft Store, Apple App Store, or another platform, that platform may process data related to download, purchase, and licensing under its own policy. This policy describes only what the Studyist app itself does.

Children's privacy

Studyist does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
