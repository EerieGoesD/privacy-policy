Deskswitch Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Deskswitch ("the app") is a desktop app from EERIE. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary

Deskswitch does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything you set up stays on your own device.

Information we collect

None. Deskswitch has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking.

Screens and display settings

To turn screens on and off, the app reads the list of screens connected to your computer from Windows, including each screen's model name and the identifier Windows gives it, and changes the Windows display settings when you switch profiles. This happens on your computer only.

Where your data is stored

All of your profiles (their names and which screens each one turns on), your hotkey and your preferences (theme, closing to the tray and whether the usage readout is shown) are saved on your own computer. They are never uploaded to us or to any server, and we have no access to them.

Network use

Deskswitch works fully offline and does not send your data anywhere. Its fonts are the ones already on your system, so no font requests are made. The only time it opens a connection is when you click a link inside the app (such as the footer links), which opens that web page in your default browser.

Third parties

Deskswitch contains no third-party trackers, advertising SDKs, or analytics libraries, and does not share data with third parties, because it collects none.

Where you get the app

If you get Deskswitch through the Microsoft Store, that platform may process data related to your download, purchase and licensing under its own privacy policy. This policy describes only what the Deskswitch app itself does.

Children's privacy

Deskswitch does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
