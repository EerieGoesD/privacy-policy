Velocimap Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Velocimap ("the app") is a phone app from EERIE that plans bike, car and walking routes. Routes are worked out on your phone, from map files you download into the app.

What we collect

From the iPhone and iPad app, nothing. From the Android app, one small record used only for the free trial: a scrambled identifier for your phone and the date and time the trial was started on it (see "Free trial and unlocking the app" below). The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your location

If you allow it, the app uses your location to show where you are, to plan routes from there and to guide you along a route. Your location is used on your phone only and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, your Home, Work and saved places, the roads you choose to avoid, your trip history and the maps you download are kept on your phone, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

Free trial and unlocking the app

Velocimap offers a free trial and then a one-time purchase for permanent access. On an iPhone or iPad the trial and the purchase are handled by the App Store; on Android the purchase is handled by Google Play. The app asks the store whether you have bought the unlock. We do not receive your name, your payment details or your Apple or Google account details, and no purchase information is sent to any server of ours.

On Android, so that the trial cannot be restarted by reinstalling the app, the date the trial started is kept on a server we run on Cloudflare. It is stored next to a scrambled identifier for your phone: a one-way hash made on the phone from the identifier Android gives this app on that device (the Android ID). The Android ID itself never leaves your phone, and the hash cannot be turned back into it. The record holds no name, email address, Google account, location or anything else about you, and it is used for nothing except the trial. Cloudflare, which runs the server, handles the connection under its own privacy policy.

We keep this record so the trial is not given twice on the same phone. Because it holds nothing that identifies you, we have no way to tell from a request which record belongs to whom.

The Android app checks the trial and the purchase with Google Play and our server when it opens, when you come back to it, from time to time while you use it, and when you start the trial, buy, or restore a purchase.

Services the app uses

Place search: when you type in the search box, your search text, and your approximate position so nearby places come first, is sent to Apple's map search, which is part of iOS, under Apple's privacy policy. The same search text, and your position rounded to about a kilometre, is also sent to Photon (photon.komoot.io), a place search built on OpenStreetMap data and run by komoot, under its own privacy policy.

Explore: when you open a kind of place nearby (for example pharmacies), that kind and your position are sent to Apple's map search, and the same kind with your position rounded to about a kilometre is sent to Overpass, a service built on OpenStreetMap data (overpass-api.de, or maps.mail.ru when that one is busy), under their own privacy policies.

Map background: the map images you see are loaded from OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org). Like any website, it receives the address of your internet connection and which part of the map is shown, under its own privacy policy.

Map downloads: the route maps are downloaded from GitHub (github.com), where they are published. GitHub receives the address of your internet connection when a map is downloaded, under its own privacy policy.

Spoken directions are made by your phone's own voice.

Links

The Report Issue, Discussions and Feature Request links in Settings open pages in your browser. Anything you choose to send there is handled under that page's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Velocimap is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Third-party distribution

If the app is obtained through the Apple App Store, Google Play or another platform, the respective platform may process data related to download, purchase and licensing under their own policies. This policy describes only processing performed by the Velocimap app itself.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
