Decathlon Product Grabber (Text & Image) Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Decathlon Product Grabber ("the app") is a desktop app from EERIE that reads a Decathlon product page and gives you its text and its picture addresses in a form you can select, copy and save. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours. It is not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Decathlon.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising. EERIE never receives the addresses you paste, the products you read, the files you save, or anything else about your computer.

What the app reads

When you press Get Product, the app opens the address you pasted in its own browser window, off screen, and reads the text already published on that page: the product title, description, product number, colour, features, design details, specifications, and the addresses of the product photographs. That text stays on your computer, in the app window, until you close the app or read another product. The app does not save it by itself.

Network use

The app is not offline software. Opening a product page is an ordinary visit to the shop's website, made from your computer, so Decathlon's servers see it the same way they would see a visit from your web browser, including your IP address. Pressing Save pictures downloads the photographs from Decathlon's picture server (mediadecathlon.com) to the folder you choose. The app only accepts addresses on decathlon, only downloads pictures from mediadecathlon.com, and contacts nothing else. It makes no connection at all when you simply open it.

Cookies from the shop's website

Because the product page is loaded in a real browser window inside the app, the shop's website can set cookies exactly as it would in your normal browser. Those cookies are stored by Windows in the app's own browser folder on your computer, are used only for loading these pages, and are never read or transmitted by the app. Removing the app removes them.

Where your settings are stored

Your settings are saved on your own computer: the light or dark theme, and whether the app usage readout under the title is shown. The addresses you paste are not saved anywhere and are gone when you close the app.

Files you save

Save as spreadsheet writes a CSV file, and Save pictures writes image files, to a location you pick each time. Those files stay on your device and under your control. The app never uploads them.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party trackers, advertising SDKs, or analytics libraries. The only third party it ever contacts is Decathlon's own website and picture server, and only for the page you asked for. What Decathlon does with that visit is covered by Decathlon's privacy policy, not this one. Links inside the app, such as "Support This Project", "Report Issue", "Discussions" and "Feature Request", open in your web browser only when you click them, taking you to third-party websites that operate under their own privacy policies.

Third-party distribution

If the app is obtained through the Microsoft Store or another platform, that platform may process data related to download, purchase, and licensing under its own policy. This policy describes only what the Decathlon Product Grabber app itself does.

Children's privacy

Decathlon Product Grabber does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
