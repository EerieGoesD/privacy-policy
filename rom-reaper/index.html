ROM Reaper Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

ROM Reaper ("the app") is a desktop app from EERIE that compresses game disc images and ROMs that are already on your computer into the formats emulators read: CHD, CSO, RVZ, XISO, trimmed 3DS and 7z. The app does not include, download or provide any games. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours, and it is not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated with any console maker or emulator project.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising. EERIE never receives the names of your files, the folders they are in, or anything else about your computer.

Your files

The app reads only the files and folders you drop on it, choose with its file picker, or paste into it. It writes each compressed copy to the folder you choose, or beside the original. If you tick the option to remove the originals, the app moves them to the Recycle Bin, and only after the new file has been read back and checked. All of this happens on your own computer. Your files are never uploaded, and the app does not keep a list of them after you close it.

The compressing is done on your computer by programs that are installed inside the app: chdman from the MAME project, DolphinTool from the Dolphin project, extract-xiso, and 7-Zip. They work on your files locally and do not connect to the internet. DolphinTool keeps a small settings folder inside the app's own data folder.

Clipboard

When you press Ctrl+V in the app's window, it reads the files or file paths on the clipboard so it can add them to the list. It does not read the clipboard at any other time, and it does not store or send what it reads.

Network use

ROM Reaper works fully offline and makes no connection at all, including when you open it. The only time a web page opens is when you click a link inside the app (such as the footer links), which opens that page in your default web browser.

Where your settings are stored

Your settings are saved on your own computer: the light or dark theme, whether the app usage readout under the title is shown, whether PSP games become CHD or CSO, the folder the new files are saved to, whether the originals are moved to the Recycle Bin, and the size and position of the app's window so it reopens the way you left it.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party trackers, advertising SDKs, or analytics libraries, and it shares no data with third parties because it collects none. Links inside the app, such as "Support This Project", "Report Issue", "Discussions" and "Feature Request", open in your web browser only when you click them, taking you to third-party websites that operate under their own privacy policies.

Third-party distribution

If the app is obtained through the Microsoft Store or another platform, that platform may process data related to download, purchase, and licensing under its own policy. This policy describes only what the ROM Reaper app itself does.

Children's privacy

ROM Reaper does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
