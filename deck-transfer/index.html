Deck Transfer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Deck Transfer ("the app") is a desktop app from EERIE that moves files between your computer and your own Steam Deck. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours. This policy covers Deck Transfer as distributed through the Mac App Store and the Microsoft Store.

What we collect

Nothing. Deck Transfer has no user accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising. We never receive your files, your Deck's details, or anything about how you use the app.

Where your data is stored

The app saves its data in its own storage folder on your computer: your settings, the Decks you saved (including the SSH password you typed for each one), your saved folder paths, the folders you gave it access to, your transfer history, your scheduled syncs, and, on macOS, a copy of your trial and purchase status. This data never leaves your computer.

Your files

Files you send or download travel directly between your computer and your Steam Deck, over your own cable or local network, encrypted by SSH. They do not pass through any server of ours or anyone else's. Downloads are saved only to the folder you choose.

Local network

To find your Deck, the app looks for devices on your local network, and on a direct Ethernet cable, that accept SSH connections. It connects only to find your Deck and to sign in to the Deck you choose.

Network use

Apart from connecting to your Steam Deck, the app works offline. Links inside the app, such as "Report Issue", "Discussions" and "Feature Request", open in your web browser only when you click them, taking you to websites that operate under their own privacy policies.

Purchases

On macOS, the free trial and the one-time purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple ID.

Third parties

Deck Transfer contains no third-party trackers, advertising SDKs, or analytics libraries.

Third-party distribution

If the app is obtained through the Microsoft Store, Apple App Store, or another platform, that platform may process data related to download, purchase, and licensing under its own policy. This policy describes only what the Deck Transfer app itself does.

Children's privacy

Deck Transfer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
