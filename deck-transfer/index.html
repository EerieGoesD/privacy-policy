Deck Transfer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Deck Transfer ("the app") is a desktop app from EERIE that moves files between your computer and your own Steam Deck. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours. This policy covers Deck Transfer as distributed through the Mac App Store and the Microsoft Store.

What we collect

Nothing. Deck Transfer has no user accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising. We never receive your files, your Deck's details, or anything about how you use the app.

Where your data is stored

The app keeps its data in its own folders on your computer:

  • your settings and the Decks you saved, including the SSH password you typed for each one;
  • your saved folder paths, and on macOS the files and folders you gave it access to;
  • your transfer history and your scheduled syncs (a scheduled sync keeps the Deck password it needs to run);
  • a log of the app's activity, such as your Deck's network address, the folders you browsed and the files you transferred, shown in the Debug window for troubleshooting;
  • on macOS, a copy of your trial and purchase status.

None of this leaves your computer.

Your files

Files you send or download travel directly between your computer and your Steam Deck, over your own cable or local network, encrypted by SSH. They do not pass through any server of ours or anyone else's. Downloads are saved only to the folder you choose.

Local network

To find your Deck, the app looks for devices on your local network, and on a direct Ethernet cable, that accept SSH connections. It tries to sign in to each of them as the user "deck" with the password you typed, over an encrypted SSH connection, until one accepts it. After that it connects only to the Deck it found.

Network use

Apart from your Steam Deck, the app connects to nothing of ours or anyone else's. On macOS, Apple's App Store checks your trial and purchase status. Links inside the app, such as "Report Issue", "Discussions" and "Feature Request", open in your web browser only when you click them, taking you to websites that operate under their own privacy policies.

Exports

Export Config writes your saved Decks (including their passwords), your saved folder paths and your settings to a file in the location you choose. That file stays on your device and under your control. The app never uploads it.

Purchases

On macOS, the free trial and the one-time purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple ID.

Third parties

Deck Transfer contains no third-party trackers, advertising SDKs, or analytics libraries.

Third-party distribution

If the app is obtained through the Microsoft Store, Apple App Store, or another platform, that platform may process data related to download, purchase, and licensing under its own policy. This policy describes only what the Deck Transfer app itself does.

Children's privacy

Deck Transfer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
