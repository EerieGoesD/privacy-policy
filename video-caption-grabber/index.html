Video Caption Grabber Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Video Caption Grabber ("the app") is a desktop app from EERIE, for Windows and Mac, that reads the captions of a YouTube video from its link and shows them as text you can copy or save. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours. It is not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated with YouTube or Google.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising. EERIE never receives the links you paste, the captions you read, the files you save, or anything else about your device.

Network use

The app is not offline software. When you paste a link or press Get Captions, the app asks YouTube, from your device, for that video's details and the list of its captions, using the yt-dlp tool built into the app. It then downloads the captions you are shown, and any others you pick, from YouTube's own servers. YouTube's servers see these requests the same way they would see a visit from your web browser, including your IP address and the video you asked about. What YouTube does with them is covered by Google's own privacy policy, not this one.

The app contacts nothing else. It makes no connection at all when you simply open it, and none until you ask for a video's captions.

What is kept on your device

So that YouTube's player does not have to be worked out again on every video, yt-dlp keeps a small cache of it in the app's own storage on your device. It holds nothing about you or the videos you read, is never sent anywhere, and removing the app removes it.

The captions and the links you paste are not saved anywhere and are gone when you close the app or read another video.

Where your settings are stored

Your settings are saved on your own device: the light or dark theme, whether the app usage readout at the bottom of the window is shown, the default caption language, whether timestamps are shown, whether each caption gets its own line, and the size and position of the app's window so it reopens the way you left it. They are never sent anywhere.

Copying and files you save

Copy Captions puts the captions on your device's clipboard. Save Captions writes a text, SRT or WebVTT file to a location you pick each time. Those files stay on your device and under your control. The app never uploads them.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party trackers, advertising SDKs, or analytics libraries. The only third party it ever contacts is YouTube, and only for the videos you ask about. Links inside the app, such as "Made by EERIE", "Report Issue", "Discussions", "Feature Request" and "Support This Project" (Windows only), open in your web browser only when you click them, taking you to websites that operate under their own privacy policies.

Third-party distribution

If the app is obtained through the Microsoft Store, the Apple App Store or another platform, that platform may process data related to download, purchase, and licensing under its own policy. This policy describes only what the Video Caption Grabber app itself does.

Children's privacy

Video Caption Grabber does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
