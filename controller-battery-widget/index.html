Controller Battery Widget Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Controller Battery Widget ("the app") is a desktop app from EERIE that shows the battery level of your game controller in a small always-on-top window. It runs entirely on your own device. The app has no accounts, no servers, and does not collect any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no sign-up, no login, and no user profile.

Controller battery readings

The app reads the battery level that a connected controller reports to your computer, over a USB cable or Bluetooth. For Xbox-type controllers it asks Windows for the battery level. This reading is shown on screen and is never stored, never leaves your device, and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Where your data lives

Your settings, such as the low-battery alert percentage and your chosen theme, are stored locally on your device in the app's own storage folder. They never leave your device.

Your control over your data

You can see and change every setting from the app's Settings panel. Uninstalling the app on Windows deletes its settings.

Payments

The free trial and the purchase are handled by the store you got the app from (the Microsoft Store or Apple's App Store) under that store's own privacy policy. The app never sees or stores your payment details.

Network use

The app itself does not connect to any server. Links inside the app (such as "Report Issue," "Discussions," and "Suggest Feature") open in your web browser only when you choose to click them, taking you to websites that operate under their own privacy policies.

What we don't collect

The app does not collect analytics, telemetry, usage data, or device information. It does not use advertising SDKs. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

Third parties

Controller Battery Widget does not share data with third parties, because it collects none. If the app is obtained through the Microsoft Store, Apple's App Store, or another platform, that platform may process data related to download, purchase, and licensing under its own policies.

Children's privacy

Controller Battery Widget does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
