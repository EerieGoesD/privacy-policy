Mailstash Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Mailstash ("the app") is a desktop app from EERIE that shows the mail of your Gmail and Microsoft accounts in one place. It talks directly to Google and Microsoft from your own computer. EERIE runs no server for it, so your mail, your addresses and your sign-ins never reach us.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no Mailstash account, and the app sends nothing to EERIE. There is no analytics, no telemetry and no tracking.

Signing in

When you add an account, the app opens Google's or Microsoft's own sign-in page in your default browser. You sign in there, and Mailstash never sees your password. Google or Microsoft then gives the app a sign-in pass for that account, which it uses to reach your mail.

For Gmail, the app asks for full access to your mail, which lets it read your email and labels, change labels (such as marking an email read), move email to the Trash and delete email in the Trash for good. For Microsoft accounts, it asks to read and change your mail, to read your email address, and to stay signed in. Mailstash never sends email. It deletes email for good only when you empty the Trash or delete emails from it, and it asks you first.

What the app does with your mail

Mailstash uses that access only to show and manage your mail when you ask it to. It lists the emails in your inbox and folders (sender, recipients, subject, a short preview, the date and whether it has been read), opens an email when you click it, marks it read when you open it, moves it to the Trash or Deleted Items when you delete it, puts it back when you click Undo, deletes emails in the Trash or Deleted Items for good when you choose Delete Selected or Empty Trash and confirm, and shows your folders or labels with their unread counts. It checks for new mail on the schedule you pick in Settings, or when you press refresh.

Your mail goes straight from Google or Microsoft to your computer. No one at EERIE can see it.

Where your data lives

The app keeps the list of accounts you added (each address, and whether it is Gmail or Microsoft) and your app settings in its own storage folder on your device. The sign-in passes are kept there too, locked with a key held in your system's own password store (Windows Credential Manager or the macOS Keychain), so the file is of no use on its own if it is copied. Your password is never stored.

The emails themselves are not saved by the app. They are held in memory while Mailstash is open and fetched again when needed. The one exception is Download: it saves the email you are reading as a file, in the place you pick in your system's Save window, and that file is then yours like any other.

How your data is protected

Every connection the app makes to Google and Microsoft is encrypted with HTTPS. The sign-in passes are encrypted on your device with AES-256, using a key kept in your system's own password store (Windows Credential Manager or the macOS Keychain), so the stored file cannot be read without your system account. The short-lived passes used to reach your mail are kept in memory only and never written to disk. Your emails are never written to disk by the app, except when you choose Download.

Because the app runs entirely on your device and EERIE runs no server for it, no Google user data is ever sent to, stored by or seen by EERIE or anyone else. Nobody at EERIE can read your mail.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Your emails, previews, labels and folder names are held in memory only while Mailstash is open. They are gone when you close the app, and the ones for an account are cleared at once when you remove it.

Your account addresses, the sign-in passes and your settings stay on your device until you delete them. Remove Account deletes that account's address and sign-in pass from your device at once. Withdrawing Mailstash's access from your Google account (myaccount.google.com/connections) stops the app reaching your Gmail straight away. Pictures from emails you open may stay in the built-in browser engine's temporary cache on your device. Downloaded email files stay where you saved them until you delete them.

EERIE keeps no Google user data at all, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete on our side. If you have a question about your data, write to the address under Contact.

Mailstash changes or deletes email in your mailbox only when you ask it to: it moves email to the Trash when you delete it, and deletes email from the Trash for good only when you choose Delete Selected or Empty Trash and confirm.

Pictures and links in emails

When you open an email, any pictures it links to are loaded from wherever the sender put them, as in other mail apps. This can let the sender know the email was opened. The app's built-in browser engine may keep those pictures in its temporary cache on your device. Links in an email open in your default browser, under that site's own privacy policy.

Network use

The app connects to Google (accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com and gmail.googleapis.com) and Microsoft (login.microsoftonline.com and graph.microsoft.com) to sign in and to reach your mail. Apart from that, it only loads the pictures in emails you open, and opens links you click, including the footer links, in your default browser.

Google user data

Mailstash's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Gmail account is used only to provide the features described above, is shown only to you inside the app, is never transferred to anyone, is never used for advertising, is never read by people, and is never used to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Data from Microsoft accounts is treated the same way.

What we don't collect

The app does not collect analytics, telemetry, usage data, or device information. The processor and memory figures it can show in its footer are read on your device and never sent anywhere. It does not use advertising SDKs. We do not sell or share your data with anyone, and we do not use it to build a profile of you.

Your control over your data

Remove Account, on an account's page in the app, deletes that account's address and sign-in pass from your device. Nothing in your mailbox changes. You can also withdraw Mailstash's access at any time from your Google account (myaccount.google.com/connections) or your Microsoft account (account.live.com/consent/Manage). Downloaded email files stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Children's privacy

Mailstash is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Third-party distribution

If the app is obtained through the Microsoft Store or another platform, the respective platform may process data related to download, purchase, and licensing under their own policies. This policy describes only processing performed by the Mailstash app itself.
