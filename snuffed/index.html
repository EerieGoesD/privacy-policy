Snuffed Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Snuffed ("the app") is an app from EERIE that counts the time since your last cigarette and shows the health milestones you reach. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours. Everything happens on your own device.

What we collect

Nothing. Snuffed has no user accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. We never see anything you enter or anything about your device.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves the date and time of your last cigarette, which trophies it has already shown you, your light or dark theme, and whether the usage line under the app's name is switched on. It also keeps a small record of your trial or purchase so it can start without waiting for the App Store. All of this stays in the app's own storage on your device and never leaves it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple ID. The app only asks the App Store whether the trial has started and whether the purchase has been made.

Network use

Apart from the App Store checks above, the app works offline. Links inside the app, such as "Made by EERIE", "Report Issue", "Discussions" and "Feature Request", open in your web browser only when you tap them, taking you to websites that operate under their own privacy policies.

Health information

The milestones in the app are general information from public health bodies and published studies, named under each one. They are not medical advice and the app does not collect or share any health data.

Third parties

Snuffed contains no third-party trackers, advertising SDKs, or analytics libraries.

Children's privacy

Snuffed does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
