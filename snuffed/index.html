Snuffed Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Snuffed ("the app") is an app from EERIE that counts the time since your last cigarette and shows the health milestones you reach. The app has no accounts and no sign-in. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What we collect

On iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows, nothing. On Android, one small record used only for the free trial: a scrambled identifier for your phone and the date and time the trial was started on it (see "Purchases" below). Snuffed has no user accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. We never see anything you enter in the app.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves the date and time of your last cigarette, the date and time of each relapse you record, which trophies it has already shown you, the numbers you enter for the money-saved counter (cigarettes a day, pack size, pack price and currency), your light or dark theme, whether trophy notifications are on, and whether the usage line under the app's name is switched on. On iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android it also keeps a small record of your trial or purchase so it can start without waiting for the store. All of this stays in the app's own storage on your device and never leaves it. Deleting the app deletes it, and Clear History in Settings removes your quit time, relapses and trophies at any time.

Notifications

On iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows and Android, if you allow notifications, the app books a reminder on your device for each trophy still to come. These are scheduled by your device itself; nothing is sent to us or to any server. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your device's notification settings.

Apple Watch

If you install the Snuffed watch app, your iPhone sends the date and time of your last cigarette to your watch over Apple's own device-to-device connection, and the watch keeps a copy so it can count while your iPhone is out of reach. Nothing else is shared, and nothing goes through us.

Purchases

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, the free trial and the one-time purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple ID. The app only asks the App Store whether the trial has started and whether the purchase has been made.

On Android, the app offers a 15-day free trial and then a one-time purchase through Google Play, which handles the payment. The app asks Google Play whether you have bought Snuffed. We never see your name, your payment details or your Google account.

So that the Android trial cannot be restarted by reinstalling the app, the date it started is kept on a server we run on Cloudflare. It is stored next to a scrambled identifier for your phone: a one-way hash made on the phone from the identifier Android gives this app on that device (the Android ID). The Android ID itself never leaves your phone, and the hash cannot be turned back into it. The record holds no name, email address, Google account or anything you enter in the app, and it is used for nothing except the trial. Because it holds nothing that identifies you, we have no way to tell which record belongs to whom.

On Windows, Snuffed is obtained through the Microsoft Store, which handles any payment. We never see your payment details or your Microsoft account, and the Windows app itself makes no store checks.

Network use

Apart from the store checks above, the app works offline. The Windows version makes no network connections of its own. The Android version connects only to Google Play and to our trial server: when it opens, when you come back to it, and when you start the trial, buy, or restore a purchase. Links inside the app, such as "Made by EERIE", "Report Issue", "Discussions" and "Feature Request", and on Windows and Android "Support This Project", open in your web browser only when you tap or click them, taking you to websites that operate under their own privacy policies.

Health information

The milestones in the app are general information from public health bodies and published studies, named under each one. They are not medical advice and the app does not collect or share any health data.

Third parties

Snuffed contains no third-party trackers, advertising SDKs, or analytics libraries. In the Android app, Google Play handles the purchase and Cloudflare runs the server that keeps the trial record, each under its own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Snuffed does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The Android trial record holds no name, contact details or account.
